01 — MULTI-AGENT AIVOIS 2.0 WINNER

Aegis AI — Disaster Response Platform

A platform that helps response teams turn incoming emergency reports into verified, prioritized and better-coordinated action.

HOW IT WORKS
  • 1. People can report an emergency through a text message or by sharing a photo. The system reads the report and helps identify what happened, where help is needed and how urgent it may be.
  • 2. Three specialist AI agents divide the work: Sentinel recognises disaster images, Oracle helps assess severity, and Logistics plans how teams and supplies can reach affected locations.
  • 3. Volunteers can verify reports before managers coordinate resources and dispatch alerts. The system is designed to keep working offline and sync updates when connectivity returns.

02 — STREAMING MLRESEARCH DEMO

Real-Time Financial Fraud Detection

A streaming system that scores transactions and helps analysts spot unusual activity and potentially connected accounts.

HOW IT WORKS
  • 1. New transaction events flow into the system through Kafka. The platform prepares useful information about the transaction so it can be evaluated quickly.
  • 2. Two machine-learning approaches work together: XGBoost looks for suspicious transaction patterns, while GraphSAGE uses relationships between accounts to add network context.
  • 3. A FastAPI service makes the risk scores available to an analyst dashboard. In the verified demo, 1,000 transactions were scored and 33 cases were flagged for review.

03 — AI RISK INTELLIGENCEDEFENSE-ONLY

RazorShield — Abuse-Ring Sentinel

A risk investigation tool that looks beyond one suspicious payment to identify patterns that may indicate coordinated abuse.

HOW IT WORKS
  • 1. It examines transaction behaviour and connections between accounts, devices, IP addresses and merchants. This helps distinguish a one-off unusual payment from a broader pattern that deserves a closer look.
  • 2. It can enrich a case with read-only payment context and use Gemini to explain the evidence. The AI explanation is advisory; it does not change the underlying risk score.
  • 3. A fixed policy gate recommends whether a case can proceed or needs review by a person. It does not automatically take financial action; decisions that affect payments stay behind human approval.
